Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

E-Shelle provides a SaaS e-commerce platform and related business tools. This policy explains how information is processed when businesses use E-Shelle, including WhatsApp Business Platform features.

WhatsApp Business Platform data

We use WhatsApp Business Platform Data to let our business customers connect and manage their own WhatsApp Business Accounts and phone numbers, and to send customer-service communications such as order confirmations, delivery updates, and support messages from those customers' own WhatsApp Business numbers. E-Shelle does not represent that it owns a customer's WhatsApp account or number.

Depending on the feature used, data may include business account, WABA and phone-number identifiers; business contact details; customer phone numbers and names; message content and delivery statuses; consent and opt-out records; and information submitted through E-Shelle forms or catalogues.

Messages are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. Businesses are responsible for having the necessary legal basis and permissions to contact their customers, using approved templates where required, and honouring opt-outs.

How we use and protect information

We use information to provide, secure, maintain, and support E-Shelle features; route messages to the correct business account; prevent abuse; and comply with legal obligations. Business and CRM data is stored on our VPS hosted by InterServer (server reference: vps3581805). We do not sell personal data. Access is limited to authorised personnel and service providers who need it to operate or support the service.

We retain information while a business account or feature requires it, or as needed for security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Businesses can request access, correction, or deletion of their information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Opt-out records may be retained as needed to respect a request not to receive further messages.

Your choices and contact

Customers who no longer wish to receive WhatsApp messages should use the opt-out instructions in the message or contact the business that sent it. Business account holders can contact E-Shelle to request assistance with their account data.
